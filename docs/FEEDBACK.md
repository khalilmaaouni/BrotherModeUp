# Feedback: your first week with BrotherMode

You are one of the first people outside this project to install BrotherMode.
As of today, nobody outside this project has ever installed it, and this
project's own capability register says so in several places. Your week is
the first outside evidence this product has. That is the whole point of
this page.

## What we are asking of you

Install BrotherMode, following the steps in README.md rather than any steps
retyped here, since retyped steps drift and README.md is the page kept
truthful by this project's own tests. Use it on one real piece of work over
the next week, not a project you invented for the occasion. When the week is
up, answer the questionnaire below and send it back.

A short note before you start: if you are on Windows, the installer refuses
to run natively; install inside WSL instead (README.md covers this). If you
use the `/plugin` install path, know that path has been run exactly once
before you, by the author, from a local copy of this repository rather than
from GitHub. Your run is the first real test of it either way, which is
useful information whether it goes smoothly or not.

## Report something the moment it breaks

Do not wait for the end of the week to tell us something broke. The moment
install fails, or something confuses you, or the product does something you
did not expect, open one of the issue templates in this repository, under
`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE`:

- `install-problem.md`, for anything broken during install.
- `friction.md`, for confusion rather than breakage: you were not sure what
  to do, or it did something other than what you expected.

A half-finished report sent the moment something breaks is worth more to us
than a polished one you write up later from memory. Paste the exact command
and the exact output. Do not tidy it up first.

## The one week questionnaire

Answer every question you can. Scale questions run 1 to 5, and both ends are
labeled in words so a bare number never has to carry the meaning by itself.
Skip a question only if it truly does not apply to you, and say why.

### Install

1. (Scale 1 to 5. 1: did not work, I could not get it running at all. 5:
   worked exactly as the instructions said, no snags.) Did the install work
   on the first try?
2. Where, if anywhere, did you hesitate or get stuck during install? Quote
   the exact step or line if you can.
3. Roughly how long did install take, start to finish? Your best estimate is
   fine.

### The first fifteen minutes

4. (Scale 1 to 5. 1: no idea, I was guessing. 5: completely obvious what to
   do next.) Once it was installed, did you know what to do next?
5. What did you expect to happen in the first fifteen minutes that did not
   happen?

### Real use

6. What did you actually use it for this week? Describe the real piece of
   work, in your own words.
7. Was there anything you tried using it for and then stopped? What was it,
   and why did you stop?

### The safety story

8. Did you ever see it refuse to do something, or stop and ask before going
   ahead? If yes, describe what happened, as exactly as you can remember it.
9. (Scale 1 to 5. 1: did not trust it, felt like it was just in my way. 5:
   trusted it completely, the reason made sense. Leave blank if question 8
   never happened to you.) If it refused or stopped you at any point, how
   much did you trust the reason it gave?
10. Did that refusal, if any, change how you worked, for better or worse?

### The writing

11. (Scale 1 to 5. 1: confusing or condescending. 5: clear and respectful
    throughout.) How did the tone of what it wrote to you feel, overall?
12. Quote one sentence, if you can find one, that was confusing, wrong, or
    that talked down to you.

### The honest killer question

13. (Scale 1 to 5. 1: no, I would not. 5: yes, without hesitation.) If
    nobody asked you to, would you keep using it next week?
14. Why, or why not? This answer matters more than any other one on this
    page. Be blunt.

### Anything else

15. Anything we did not think to ask: a rough edge, a pleasant surprise, or
    a question you had that this page did not answer.

## What we will do with your answers, and what we will not

This process collects nothing automatically. No telemetry is gathered from
your machine, and nothing is sent to us unless you type it and submit it
yourself, either into the issue template or directly to whoever sent you
this link. We do not read your files, your BrotherMode project folder, or
anything else on your machine to fill in gaps in what you tell us.

We will use your answers to decide what to fix before anyone else installs
this, and to write down, honestly, what a first week outside this project
actually looked like. We will not share your answers with anyone outside
this project, and we will not quote them anywhere public, without asking you
first.

## How to send it back

Two ways, your choice. Paste your answers into the `week-one-feedback.md`
issue template in this repository and submit it, or send them directly to
whoever sent you the link to this project. Either way is fine. There is no
third option, and no form, email address, or chat channel we have not told
you about on this page.
