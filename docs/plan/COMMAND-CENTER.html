Risk management, insights and alerts 4

The differentiation is confirmed by the platform's own documentation

What
Claude Code ships per-plugin detail views, context-cost estimates and an unused-plugin flag, and performs NO cross-plugin diff and NO conflict detection. Confirmed absent from the official plugin docs, URL in the plan.
Why
It means F2 fills a real gap the platform owner has left open, rather than racing the platform on discovery, which is the race the marketplace decision refuses.
Action
None from you. It is written into the plan with its source.
When
Re-verify the gap still exists at each Claude Code major release; the platform closing it is the plan's biggest external risk.

Toolkit is new-subsystem work, which is where forecasts break

What
Last night's calibration says well-specified work runs about 2 to 4 times faster than judged, and the ONE task that hit unforeseen ground ran slower than judged, 0.66x. Every Toolkit loop parses surfaces nobody here has parsed before.
Why
The multiplier tracks specification quality, not size. Toolkit forecasts are therefore stated RAW, no fast multiplier applied, per the rule captured last night pending your approval.
Action
Nothing now. Each loop records forecast and actual; the sample will say whether toolkit-class work earns its own multiplier.
When
Reforecast after T1 and T2 land, the first two real data points on this class.

Hookify ordering is undocumented, so one conflict class needs an experiment

What
Research confirmed Hookify rules land on the same PreToolUse and Stop surface as every other plugin, and cross-plugin ordering and block-versus-allow collision behavior is undocumented.
Why
F2 wants to report what HAPPENS when two blockers disagree, and for this class the truth is not in any file; it needs a controlled experiment on a throwaway config.
Action
T2 includes one measured experiment: two deliberately conflicting hooks, observed resolution, written into the conflict-classes file as measured fact.
When
During T2, this week.

BMAD's on-disk footprint is unconfirmed, so its route stays disabled

What
The research pass could not confirm what state files BMAD-METHOD writes. Every other researched tool has a named namespace.
Why
A route to a tool whose state footprint is unknown would put an unmeasured conflict surface inside the product that sells conflict detection.
Action
The BMAD route ships in the seed table marked DISABLED with this reason; a follow-up repo-tree read enables it.
When
Before T4 closes.
