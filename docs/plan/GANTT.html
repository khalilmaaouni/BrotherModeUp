The multiplier is not about size. It is about how well specified the work is

What
At n=7 the median is 3.75x but the spread widened from 3.75-to-5.33 to 0.66-to-5.33. That 0.66 is R1.1: forecast 45 minutes, actual 68. It ran slower than judged, and it was the one task tonight that hit a problem nobody had foreseen, the canonical shape refusing the new fields.
Why
This is the plan's own root-cause claim being tested against itself and surviving. An agent is roughly four times faster than a person on work whose files and done-check are already named, and at or below parity on work it has to figure out first, because it pays full price for every wrong turn. So a single multiplier applied to everything would have been the old mistake with a decimal point on it.
Action
Nothing from you tonight. From now on a task whose shape is not yet known gets forecast raw, with the multiplier explicitly not applied and the unspecified part named. That is captured as a pending rule for your approval, not self-approved.
When
At the Monday review, with n past twelve, split the sample by specified and unspecified and see whether the two populations really separate.
