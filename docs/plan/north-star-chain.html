The operating model

Intent to Verified Reality

One chain, eight stages, and a human decision that no automation may remove. This is the north star for what gets built and how it gets built.

The colour on each stage is where that stage stands today, not where it is meant to end up. Four of the last five do not exist yet, and saying so is the point: a diagram that only draws the finished shape hides the work.

Verified reality
Does not exist

Whether it actually worked, observed rather than asserted. The terminal state, and deliberately not “the gate went green”. A green gate is a claim about proof. Verified reality is a claim about the world. Nothing computes it today, and the agreed success measure cannot even be recomputed.

The two rules this imposes on everything

Rule one: the human loop is never optimised away

Humans stay in the loop at four points: the intent that starts the chain, any forcing condition where guessing is the danger, the release decision, and the acceptance that closes it. A change that removes, bypasses or silently pre-answers one of those is refused, whatever it saves.

This is a mechanism, not an intention. A stuck run now converts itself into a named decision for a person, with a recommendation and a default action, on four triggers: a danger where guessing is the risk, three distinct approaches failed, two failures sharing one root cause, or a spent budget. It never answers the question itself and never decides on anyone’s behalf.

Rule two: every item of work names its stage

Work that cannot name the stage it serves goes to the parking lot, not the backlog. This is now checked rather than remembered: the queue knows the fourteen stage names, an item naming one that does not exist fails hard and names the item, and an item naming none is reported on every run without breaking anything. Today 37 queued items name no stage, and the check says so out loud.

Where the gaps came from, which is the part worth trusting

Nine holes were found earlier the same day by walking the lifecycle and asking what has no tool at all, before this chain was written down. Five of the nine turned out to be one hole seen five times: the system is complete up to the merge and blind after it.

This chain names that same hole from the opposite direction. Its last four stages are exactly the four that do not exist. The gaps are not scattered debt, they are the unbuilt half of the model, and two independent passes reached it within an hour of each other.
