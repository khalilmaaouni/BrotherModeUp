# The whole roadmap, ranked by what it unblocks

## The question I ranked against

The north star is intent to a verified, review-ready deliverable with bounded
autonomy, independent proof, and recoverable state. Against that, the project
has three real gaps and everything below is scored on which one it closes:

- NOBODY OUTSIDE HAS USED IT. The external proof rung is empty and says so.
- ADOPTION IS BLOCKED AT THE FIRST ARTIFACT. The analysis of 2026-08-11 shows
  the barrier is social rather than technical, and the current first artifact
  is a hosted link, which is the one shape that cannot be forwarded as
  somebody's own work.
- AUTONOMY IS NOT YET SAFE TO LEAVE ALONE. Ratified decision 27 says no
  unattended stretches until the stall sentinel catches a seeded stall end to
  end, and it does not yet exist in a landed form.

Anything that closes one of those outranks anything that does not, however
mature or nearly finished it is.

## Rank 1: adoption L1, the file that opens with no account

WHY FIRST: it is half a day, and every other adoption loop delivers through it.
Until it is true, every artifact this product hands somebody is a request
rather than a gift. It is also the cheapest item on this whole list per unit of
unblocking. IN FLIGHT as of this writing.
DONE-CHECK: a test asserting zero fetched external references, calibrated by
injecting one; file under five megabytes.

may simply overrule this; it is his priority and my ranking is advice.

## Two items shrank when checked, and the pattern is worth more than either

Twice on 2026-08-11 an item on this roadmap turned out to be already built,
and both times the discovery came from checking rather than from planning.

- Adoption L1, the self contained file, was called the blocker that everything
  else waited on. The renderer already produced one: verified on a file it
  actually wrote, 23510 bytes, no fetched images, styles, fonts or data. What
  remained was documenting it and changing a delivery habit.
- Adoption L5, the measurement, was planned as a counter to build. The store
  already carries a views table recording every page render with its project,
  time and actor. What remains is a reader over existing rows.

THE PATTERN: this roadmap was written from what the product FELT like it was
missing rather than from what it demonstrably lacked. Both corrections cost
minutes to find and would have cost days to build. Every remaining item on this
list should be checked the same way before it is started, and the check belongs
in the first step of the loop rather than in a review afterwards.

That also means the ranking above is less trustworthy than it reads. It is
ordered by what each item would unblock IF the item is real, and two of eleven
were not.

## What I am NOT proposing, and why

No new capability that serves none of the three gaps. The board already carries
a written will-not-build list, and the discipline that list represents is worth
more than any item that could be added to it. An addition that cannot name
which gap it closes belongs in a parking lot rather than in this file.
