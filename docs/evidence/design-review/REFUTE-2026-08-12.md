## What this changes

The four design documents are committed to main and were about to drive R2 and
R3 work. They are NOT retracted by this report, and nothing here says the
recommendations are wrong. What it says is narrower and more useful: at least
two of them did not genuinely contest the design they compete with, and one
rests on a mechanism this repository's own gates refuse. Those are cheap to fix
now and expensive to discover after code assumes them.

Recommended, and left for the founder rather than taken: the memory document
gets a third rejected alternative, namely "extend `learning_rules`", argued
against properly, before anything is built on it.
