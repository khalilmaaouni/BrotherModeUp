ceiling, and the engine charges the meter BEFORE it judges the result
(REFUTATION-3 SM C moved the charge there deliberately, so a late result

than an exception: _breaker_is_this_results_own_cost. The breaker refuses
every command unless subtracting the spend row THIS call just wrote for
THIS unit puts both meters back under their ceilings, in which case the
one already-paid unit in flight is still judged. The refuted sequence is
untouched (REFUTATION-5 S2 blew the ceiling with a separate
`bm-autonomy spend` BEFORE record-result, and supplied no cost at all, so
there is nothing to subtract), and the run still drains on the next step
and still cannot deliver. The residual is disclosed in
docs/KNOWN-LIMITS.md and in section 3 of the round-6 report.
