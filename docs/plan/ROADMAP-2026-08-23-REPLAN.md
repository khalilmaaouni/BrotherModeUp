# The roadmap, replanned 2026-08-23: finish the backlog, prepare, then one Brother repository

## 1. GOAL, in the founder's words of 2026-08-22

ONE Brother repository holding the three products: BrotherMode (execution provenance), BrotherSBE (assurance, the eight concerns, the change passport as the seam) and BrotherDS (the data-science colleague that measures whether reality agreed). Merged at the right time, not now. FIRST the backlog is finished properly; MEANWHILE each repository is prepared so the merge is a move, not a rewrite.

The laws that bound it and that this plan may not weaken: PRODUCT-DIRECTION.md is founder-owned authority (the merge enters it as a PROPOSED amendment he lands); a merged public repository is a clean extraction of each tree at a chosen commit, never a history merge and never a scrub; no plugin is a dependency of another; the passport is the seam, not a schema; the tool surface is frozen until the four user journeys pass; two hosts for every development; no self-firing CI.
